# C1 — Sprout creature family

Status: Design proposal awaiting user review, 29 October 2026. The user approved proceeding with the correction → creature → gameplay expansion sequence. No final creature model or rig is claimed complete.

## Identity proposal

One original woodland drake family. A compact quadruped with a defined muzzle, brow/cheeks, four articulated legs and broad three-toed paws, a tapered expressive tail, leaf-shaped ears and a small botanical forehead crest. Warm cream underside, moss-green body and restrained foliage accents. Body, head, neck, limbs and tail form an intentional silhouette; no finished form may be an assembly of visible balls. Keep the face readable at normal companion scale.

Baby: curious, low stance, larger head relative to body, short crest and tail. Young: longer torso/tail, stronger limbs and more developed crest. Evolved forms keep the same recognizable anatomy. Fire/Ember Sprout uses warm rust/copper plates and ember crest; Crystal Sprout uses lavender mineral ridges and pale aqua accents; Nature/Bloom Sprout uses deeper green leaf mantle, branching crest and small peach flowers. Equal gameplay viability; no paid stronger branch.

Working avatar-scale targets: baby about 2.2 studs high/3 long, young 3.2 high/4.7 long, evolved around 3.7 high/5.5 long. Review beside an avatar before locking dimensions. The egg is a seed-like shell with overlapping botanical bands, sized to plausibly contain the curled baby.

## Deliverables in order

1. Starter front/side/back design views plus evolution direction sheet. Generated concept art is reference only, not an imported/rigged asset.
2. User approves anatomy, silhouette and palette. Resolve inconsistent generated views in the modeling spec before mesh production.
3. Build ONE baby as a genuine mesh with readable face, limbs/paws/tail and materials; inspect in Studio at avatar scale, from front/side/back and close view.
4. Rig and validate the baby in idle/turn/walk poses. This is the pipeline gate before reproducing the family.
5. Build egg, young and three evolved meshes using that validated family language, UV/material treatment and rig conventions.
6. C2 supplies hatch, locomotion, feeding, growth, delight and evolution animation, plus grounded following on slopes/bridges.

## Modeling route and acceptance

Use original custom mesh authoring/import or inspected compatible free assets where necessary. Existing EditableMesh authoring proves local geometry generation, not automatic production-quality organic modeling/rigging. Inspect available tools at modeling time. If external modeling is needed, identify the exact missing deliverable and preserve the approved design; do not silently substitute a sphere or label a weak model finished. Record topology/UV/material/rig sources and rights. No paid asset or publication without authorization.

This sheet does not replace actual models. Required acceptance: consistent anatomy, mirrored limb placement, usable joints/deformation, no floating feet, no clipping on turn/feed, readable expressions, controlled growth and appropriate collision/camera scale. Device performance and multiplayer replication must be tested.

## Scope discipline

After the current W4 corrections, start the baby mesh pipeline. Do not keep adding unrelated environment ornaments instead of moving to the creature. UI and environmental additions should support that vertical slice, not delay it indefinitely.
