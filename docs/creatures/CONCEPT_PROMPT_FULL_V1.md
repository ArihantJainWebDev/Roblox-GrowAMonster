Wide landscape concept-board composition, neutral warm ivory background, clear spacing, restrained typography, crisp polished hand-painted 3D game-character concept style with simple MODELABLE forms and matte surfaces, not photoreal, no environment.

Moss-green skin with warm cream underside, subtle darker green leaf mantle along back. The creature must have intentional anatomy, not a ball body with disconnected balls for limbs. Cute and curious, but visually crafted, not a toy blob. No resemblance to any specific existing franchise or trademark creature. No wings, no armor clutter, no weapons.

Mature about 3.7 studs high / 5.5 long; preserve a believable progression from young form. Avoid excessive glowing effects that hide anatomy.
A small separate botanical SEED EGG inset in an upper corner: tapered seed-pod shell, cream-green overlapping leaf bands, no face. Label "SEED EGG". Keep it distinct from the creature turnaround grid.
Small row labels "BABY SPROUT" and "YOUNG SPROUT", and view captions "FRONT", "SIDE", "BACK". Include one tiny plain gray block-avatar silhouette as a scale reference, not a branded character.
Maintain flat ground baselines and orthographic-like turnarounds, all body parts in frame. No dramatic perspective in turnaround rows. Consistent leg count, toe structure, ear positions and tail attachment. Keep the board readable and uncluttered. This is a design proposal for modeling, not an image of a finished Roblox game.
