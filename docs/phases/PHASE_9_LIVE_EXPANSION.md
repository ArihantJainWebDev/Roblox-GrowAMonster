# Phase 9 — Post-Release Expansion

**Status:** Planned; begins only after a stable first release and player feedback.

## Goal

Expand the game without destabilizing existing saves or weakening the original cozy progression.

## Candidate work

- Additional regions, foods, crops, monster forms, decorations, and quests.
- Social island visits and co-op goals if they are requested and can be safely bounded.
- Trading, combat, breeding, competitive features, or seasonal content only after separate design and risk review.

## Exit checks for each update

- Existing profiles migrate safely and keep their chosen evolution and items.
- New content has a clear place in the economy and does not make earlier content obsolete without reason.
- Update can be released and rolled back in a bounded way; player-facing changes are documented.
